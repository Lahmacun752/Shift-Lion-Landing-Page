Understand the result

What does this tool calculate?

The calculator maps entered working time to the general break thresholds under the German Working Time Act and shows total attendance including the minimum break.

Example

For 8 hours of working time, the general minimum break is 30 minutes, resulting in 8 hours 30 minutes of attendance.

How it is calculated

Up to and including 6 hours: no statutory minimum break. More than 6 up to 9 hours: 30 minutes. More than 9 hours: 45 minutes.

What is not included?

Special rules can apply to young workers, particular sectors, collective agreements and company policies. This tool does not assess an individual employment arrangement.

Frequently asked questions

Does a break count as working time?

An unpaid rest break generally does not count as working time and therefore extends attendance.

Can the break be split?

Under the general German rule, breaks may be split into periods of at least 15 minutes each.

Is a break required at exactly six hours?

Under the general threshold, the statutory minimum begins only when working time exceeds six hours.

Does this apply to minors?

No. Young workers are subject to separate German youth-employment rules.
