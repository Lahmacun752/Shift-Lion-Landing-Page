CALCULATION GUIDE

Understand the result

What does this tool calculate?

The calculator separates regular, holiday and optional Sunday hours and estimates base pay, allowances and gross total.

Example

7:30 paid holiday hours at €20 and a 100% holiday rate produce €150 base pay plus €150 holiday allowance.

How it is calculated

Holiday hours × hourly wage × holiday rate = holiday allowance. For Sunday overlaps, your selected combination rule is used.

What is not included?

The tool does not determine whether a date legally qualifies as a holiday or verify regional, contractual and tax rules.

Frequently asked questions

How are overnight shifts divided?

Choose whether the shift starts on, leads into or falls fully within the holiday.

What happens with an unpaid break?

It is deducted from base pay and every enabled allowance.

Can Sunday and holiday overlap?

Yes. Choose the higher rate, add both rates or use a custom combined rate.
