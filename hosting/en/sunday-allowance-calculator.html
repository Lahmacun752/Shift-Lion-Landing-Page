CALCULATION GUIDE

Understand the result

What does this tool calculate?

The calculator multiplies paid Sunday hours by hourly wage and your own premium rate, showing base pay, Sunday premium and estimated gross total separately.

Example

Eight Sunday hours at €20 produce €160 base pay. A 50% premium adds €80, resulting in an estimated €240 gross total.

How it is calculated

Sunday hours × hourly wage = base pay. Base pay × premium rate = Sunday premium. Adding both values gives the estimated gross total.

What is not included?

A Sunday premium is not automatically guaranteed at a particular rate. Entitlement, compensatory rest and tax treatment depend on the applicable rules.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my own percentage?

Yes. Enter the rate from your contract, collective agreement or company policy.

Is the premium automatically tax-free?

No. The calculator does not assess tax treatment and displays gross values only.

How should unpaid breaks be handled?

Enter only the Sunday hours that are actually paid.

Can a night premium apply as well?

Possibly, but whether premiums may be combined depends on your applicable agreement.
