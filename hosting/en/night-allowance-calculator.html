CALCULATION GUIDE

Understand the result

What does this tool calculate?

The calculator finds paid hours inside your chosen night period and estimates base pay and night allowance.

Example

For 22:00–06:00, a 30-minute unpaid break, €20 hourly pay and 25%, the result is 7:30 paid night hours, €150 base pay and €37.50 allowance.

How it is calculated

Paid night hours × hourly wage × allowance rate = night allowance. An unpaid break removes base pay and all allowances for that time.

What is not included?

Contractual night periods, tax treatment and collective-agreement rules are not checked automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change the night period?

Yes. The eligible start and end times are adjustable.

Does an unpaid break reduce night allowance?

Yes. It reduces paid time and eligible night hours.

Can Sunday allowance be included?

Yes. Enable Sunday work and enter your own percentage.
