What counts as working time?

Gross time is the period between the start and end of work. Subtracting the entered break gives you net working time. The balance compares this with your target hours for each completed working day.

CALCULATION GUIDE

Understand the result

What does this tool calculate?

The calculator uses shift start, end and break times to show gross time, net working time and the difference from your target hours.

Example

A 06:00–14:00 shift is 8 hours gross. After a 30-minute unpaid break, 7 hours 30 minutes remain.

How it is calculated

Shift end − shift start − unpaid break = net working time. Net time − target time = overtime balance.

What is not included?

It does not verify collective agreements, rounding rules, on-call time or every legal exception.

Frequently asked questions

Does it support overnight shifts?

Yes. An end time before the start is treated as the following day.

How are paid breaks handled?

When paid breaks are enabled, the break remains part of net working time.

Is this an official time record?

No. It is a personal calculation aid, not an employer time record.
