Understand the result

What does this tool calculate?

The calculator converts annual leave from a reference workweek to your actual regular workdays per week and can estimate a proportion for full months employed.

Example

Thirty days of leave in a five-day reference week correspond to 18 days when you regularly work three days per week for a full year.

How it is calculated

Reference annual leave ÷ reference workdays per week × actual workdays per week. For a partial year, the result is additionally weighted by full months employed.

What is not included?

Contracts, collective agreements, statutory waiting periods, start and end dates, rounding and leave already taken can change the actual entitlement.

Frequently asked questions

Why is leave converted by workdays?

The conversion aims to preserve a comparable number of weeks away from work across schedules.

How do I enter a rotating schedule?

Use the average number of regular workdays per week across the rotation.

Is the partial-year result legally binding?

No. The month field is a planning estimate and does not assess every statutory exception.

Are public holidays deducted as leave?

The calculator does not make that determination; applicable local rules and the roster must be checked separately.
