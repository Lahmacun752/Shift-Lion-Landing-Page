Understand the result

What does this tool calculate?

The calculator combines early, late, night, Sunday and public-holiday hours to estimate monthly base pay, separate premiums and gross total.

Example

At €20 per hour, 160 hours produce €3,200 base pay. Forty night hours with a 25% premium add €200 before any other premiums.

How it is calculated

Total hours × hourly wage = base pay. For each premium: eligible hours × hourly wage × entered percentage. Base pay plus all premiums gives the estimated gross total.

What is not included?

The tool does not verify contracts, collective agreements, tax exemptions, social contributions or whether overlapping premiums may be combined.

Frequently asked questions

Can I enter my own rates?

Yes. Every premium percentage can be adjusted to match your applicable agreement.

Is the result take-home pay?

No. It is a non-binding gross estimate before deductions.

Can hours belong to more than one premium category?

The calculation permits it, but your contract or collective agreement determines whether premiums may be combined.

Should unpaid breaks be included?

No. Enter only hours that count as paid working time.
