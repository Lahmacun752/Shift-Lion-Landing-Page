Frequently asked questions

Does Shift Lion create the employer roster?

No. Your workplace remains responsible for staff scheduling; the app is for your personal overview.

Can planning reduce shift-work stress?

A clear overview cannot remove every burden, but it can reduce uncertainty and last-minute conflicts.
