Understand the result

What does this tool calculate?

The calculator measures the time between the end of one shift and the start of the next and compares it with Germany's general eleven-hour rest rule.

Example

If one shift ends at 22:00 and the next begins at 09:00 the following day, the rest period is exactly 11 hours.

How it is calculated

Start of the next shift minus end of the previous shift = rest period. The result is compared with the general eleven-hour reference.

What is not included?

German working-time law includes sector-specific exceptions and compensation rules. On-call duty, collective agreements and individual cases are not assessed automatically.

Frequently asked questions

What if the result is below eleven hours?

The calculator shows the shortfall. You still need to check whether a permitted exception applies.

Does it work across midnight?

Yes. Full dates and times are used, so different calendar days are handled correctly.

Does commuting count as rest time?

The tool only measures the entered interval and does not make a legal assessment of commuting time.

Is eleven hours always mandatory?

It is the general German rule, but legislation and collective agreements allow exceptions in certain sectors.
