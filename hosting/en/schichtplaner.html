What is a personal shift planner?

A personal shift planner helps workers organize early, late and night shifts, rotating schedules and days off. When working times change throughout the week, a digital shift calendar can be easier to maintain than a paper diary or a spreadsheet.

Shift Lion lets you save your own repeating rotation and plan work, leave, appointments and free time in one place.

Shift planner, employer roster and time clock: the difference

Personal shift planner

Copy your own assigned shifts into a personal calendar and organize life around them.

Employer roster

Your employer remains responsible for assigning staff and publishing the official roster. Shift Lion does not replace workforce-management software.

Not a live time clock

Shift Lion evaluates planned times stored with your shifts. It is not a clock-in and clock-out attendance system.

Suitable for — and not designed for

Shift Lion is deliberately a personal planner. This distinction helps you decide whether it fits your purpose.

Shift Lion is suitable for

  • individual shift workers using Android
  • early, late, night and rotating shifts
  • custom rotations and several shift groups
  • leave, appointments and personal changes
  • finding shared days off
  • personal working-time and pay estimates

Shift Lion is not designed for

  • employer workforce planning
  • assigning shifts to an entire workforce
  • staff approval and leave-request workflows
  • live clock-in and clock-out attendance tracking

Frequently asked questions

Is Shift Lion an employer scheduling app?

No. Shift Lion is a personal planner for individual shift workers. It does not create or publish official staff rosters for employers.

Can I create my own rotation?

Yes. Define custom shift types, times and repeating patterns with flexible start dates.

Can I manage and compare several schedules?

Yes. Multiple shift groups can be saved and compared to find shared time off.

Is Shift Lion a time-tracking app?

It summarizes planned working times based on saved shifts, but it is not a live clock-in and clock-out system.

Does Shift Lion calculate pay?

The app provides a non-binding gross and take-home-pay orientation based on your entries. It does not replace payroll or professional tax, legal or financial advice.

Does it work offline and without an account?

Yes. Personal planning can be used locally without a mandatory account or cloud connection.

Is Shift Lion free?

Shift Lion is free to install. Optional premium functions are available through Google Play as in-app purchases.
